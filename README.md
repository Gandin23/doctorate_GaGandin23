\# 🌊 Freshwater IMTA &amp; Phosphorus Dynamics Data Repository Welcome! 👋 This repository contains the raw bibliographic datasets and search strategies supporting research on \*\*Phosphorus (P) Mass Balance, Sediment Dynamics, and Biogeochemical Modeling in Freshwater Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture (IMTA)\*\*. This open-science repository was built to ensure transparency, reproducibility, and easy data access for researchers, students, and practitioners interested in sustainable aquaculture. --- ## 💡 About The Project Freshwater aquaculture plays a critical role in global food security, yet phosphorus accumulation in pond sediments remains a major environmental challenge. Integrating benthic and pelagic extractive species (IMTA) offers a path toward nutrient circularity. This dataset aggregates \*\*1,091 peer-reviewed records\*\* from the \*Web of Science\* across six thematic pillars: - 🔄 \*\*Sedimentation &amp; Resuspension\*\* - 🔮 \*\*Predictive Modeling &amp; Water Quality\*\* - 🚧 \*\*System Limitations &amp; Challenges\*\* - 🌿 \*\*Ecological &amp; Trophic Interactions\*\* - 🧮 \*\*Biogeochemical &amp; Process Modeling\*\* - ⏱️ \*\*Temporal Dynamics &amp; Biogeochemistry\*\* ---
