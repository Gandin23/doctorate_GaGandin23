
# Freshwater IMTA & Environmental Dynamics
### Doctoral Research | Literature Mapping, Data Analysis & Biogeochemical Modeling

This repository supports the development of a doctoral research project on environmental processes in freshwater aquaculture ponds, with an initial focus on integrated multi-trophic aquaculture (IMTA), sediment dynamics, nutrient cycling, and quantitative modeling.

Building on previous research on phosphorus mass balance in earthen ponds, this project explores broader questions concerning the spatial and temporal dynamics of aquaculture ecosystems. The current literature-mapping phase aims to identify knowledge gaps, methodological limitations, competing hypotheses, and promising research directions before the doctoral research questions are finalized.

The repository currently contains **1,091 bibliographic records exported from the Web of Science across six thematic searches**. These records provide the starting point for a structured exploration of the scientific literature.

---

## 🎯 Research Scope

The literature mapping explores six interconnected research areas:

1. **Sedimentation and Bottom Morphology** — Sediment accumulation, transport, erosion, resuspension, spatial heterogeneity, and methods for measuring changes in pond-bottom elevation.
2. **Prediction and Environmental Monitoring** — Predictive approaches, early-warning systems, water-quality forecasting, and the identification of environmental indicators.
3. **Research Limitations and Knowledge Gaps** — Methodological constraints, measurement uncertainty, environmental impacts, operational challenges, and unresolved scientific questions.
4. **Ecological Interactions** — Benthic–pelagic coupling, bioturbation, food-web interactions, microbial processes, and ecological complementarity in IMTA systems.
5. **Modeling and Quantitative Methods** — Dynamic models, mass-balance approaches, mechanistic modeling, simulation, parameter estimation, and model validation.
6. **Temporal Dynamics** — Diurnal, seasonal, and long-term variability in environmental processes, sediment properties, and biogeochemical fluxes.

These themes are exploratory rather than predefined doctoral objectives. Their purpose is to support evidence-based research design and identify questions that are scientifically relevant, testable, and feasible.

### Potential Research Directions

The literature may inform future investigations into:

- Interactions between sediment dynamics and nutrient cycling.
- Spatial and temporal variability in pond environmental conditions.
- The influence of benthic organisms on sediment–water interactions.
- The reliability of conventional sampling and mass-balance methods.
- The development and validation of quantitative models for aquaculture ecosystems.
- The transfer of research findings into practical monitoring tools and management applications.

The final research questions, hypotheses, and methodological approaches will be defined based on the evidence gathered and evaluated during the literature review.

## 🔬 Research Workflow

The repository is intended to support a transparent and reproducible research workflow.

1. **Literature Retrieval** — Collect bibliographic records using documented search strategies.
2. **Data Organization and Deduplication** — Standardize records, identify duplicates, and maintain traceability between source exports and the consolidated database.
3. **Screening and Evidence Extraction** — Evaluate study relevance and document research questions, methods, findings, and limitations.
4. **Research Gap Identification** — Compare findings across studies, identify unresolved questions, and distinguish documented limitations from potential research opportunities.
5. **Hypothesis Development** — Formulate competing explanations and identify measurements or experiments capable of testing them.
6. **Quantitative Analysis and Modeling** — Develop computational workflows where justified by the research questions and available evidence.

Python and R may be used for bibliographic processing, statistical analysis, visualization, and modeling. Specific methods will be selected according to the needs of the research.
