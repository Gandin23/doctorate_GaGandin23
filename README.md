\# 🌊 Freshwater IMTA &amp; Phosphorus Dynamics: Doctoral Research Repository <p align="center">     </p> --- ## 📌 Overview Welcome to the official data and computational repository supporting the doctoral research project on \*\*Phosphorus (P) Mass Balance, Sediment-Water Interface Dynamics, and Biogeochemical Modeling in Freshwater Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture (IMTA)\*\*. Building upon empirical mass balance findings from earthen pond ecosystems, this research transitions from static nutrient budgets toward \*\*dynamic, predictive biogeochemical modeling\*\*—focusing on benthic-pelagic coupling, bioturbation by benthic extractive species, and sediment phosphorus fractionation. This repository hosts the \*\*1,091 systematic literature records\*\* retrieved from the \*Web of Science\* database across six core scientific pillars to ensure full methodological reproducibility and open-data access. --- ## 🎯 Research Objectives &amp; Pillars 1\. \*\*Quantify Benthic-Pelagic Coupling\*\*: Evaluate how bioturbation by \*Macrobachium rosenbergii\* (giant freshwater prawn) and \*Prochilodus lineatus\* (curimbatá) influences sediment resuspension, redox potential, and soluble reactive phosphorus (SRP) fluxes. 2\. \*\*Dynamic Biogeochemical Modeling\*\*: Develop mechanistically coupled pelagic-benthic models in Python/R to simulate continuous temporal variability in dissolved oxygen, pH, and phosphorus pools. 3\. \*\*Optimizing Nutrient Circularity\*\*: Identify ecological tipping points to maximize Phosphorus Use Efficiency (PUE) and reduce environmental discharge in aquaculture effluents. --- ## 📊 Systematic Review Datasets (Web of Science) The table below summarizes the six dataset files stored in the [\`Bases\_dados\_revisao2026/\`](./Bases\_dados\_revisao2026) directory: | Thematic Pillar | Dataset File | Records | Core Focus &amp;
